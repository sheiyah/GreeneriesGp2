WHY ARE PLANTS IMPORTANT?

Plants Give Us Life

Plants are an essential part of life on Earth. They produce oxygen through photosynthesis, provide food and habitats for animals, and help maintain healthy ecosystems. Plants also absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere and contribute to a healthier environment.

PROPER TREE PLANTING

Proper tree planting helps trees establish strong roots and grow successfully. First, choose a suitable location with enough space, sunlight and access to water. Dig a hole that is wide enough for the roots and avoid planting the tree too deeply. Place the tree upright, gently cover the roots with soil, and water it thoroughly. Adding mulch around the base can help retain moisture, but it should not touch the trunk. Regular care during the early stages of growth gives the tree a better chance of surviving.

01

02

03

Water regularly and monitor the tree while it becomes established.

PLANTS & IGCSE BIOLOGY

Plants are an important part of IGCSE Biology. Understanding photosynthesis, plant structures, transport and reproduction helps us understand how plants survive and grow.

Explore Biology

KEY PLANT TOPICS

Photosynthesis

Plants use light energy to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen.

CO₂ + H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + O₂

Transpiration

Water evaporates from the leaves of a plant, mainly through tiny openings called stomata.

Transport

Xylem transports water and mineral ions from the roots, while phloem transports dissolved sugars around the plant.

Plant Reproduction

Flowering plants reproduce using structures such as flowers, pollen and ovules, eventually producing seeds.
